    Open questions — living log

    A single index of the hypothesis-labeled open questions scattered across the playbook, so they are easy to find, revisit, and promote to "Confirmed against source" as they get verified against the rippled/ tree.

    This is a living document. When you add a new open question to a section, add a row here. When you verify a hypothesis against source, move it to Recently resolved and update the label in its section.

    How to read the status column

    • 🔬 Hypothesis — a reasoned answer that has not been verified against rippled/ source. Highest priority to resolve.
    • 🧭 Educated guess — an operational/best-practice answer that is not directly encoded in the protocol source (so it cannot be "confirmed against source" the same way), but reflects informed judgement.
    • ❓ Unanswered — a plain question with no answer written yet.
    • ✅ Confirmed — verified against source; tracked in Recently resolved, not the open list below.
